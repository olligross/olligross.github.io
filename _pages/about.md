<div class="home-updates"><p>I am a postdoctoral researcher at <a href="https://ucsd.edu">UC San Diego</a>, working with <a href="https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~alchern/">Albert Chern</a>. My research lies at the interface of physics simulation and geometric PDEs, where I use tools from (discrete) differential geometry, geometric mechanics, and the calculus of variations to develop structure-preserving discretizations and algorithms for problems in applied and computational mathematics, physical simulation, geometry processing, and robotics.</p><p>Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at <a href="https://www.epfl.ch/de/">EPFL</a> with <a href="https://people.epfl.ch/mark.pauly">Mark Pauly</a> and an investigator in the <a href="https://www.dfg.de/de">DFG</a> research center SFB/TRR 109, <a href="https://www.discretization.de">“Discretization in Geometry and Dynamics”</a> at <a href="https://www.math.tu-berlin.de/menue/home/parameter/en/">TU Berlin</a>, where I received my PhD supervised by <a href="https://page.math.tu-berlin.de/~pinkall/">Ulrich Pinkall</a> and <a href="http://users.cms.caltech.edu/~ps/">Peter Schröder</a>, including a research stay with the latter at <a href="https://www.caltech.edu">Caltech</a>.</p><aside class="job-market-note" aria-label="Job market"><strong>Job market</strong><span>I’m on the 2026–2027 academic job market and also open to research roles in industry. <a href="mailto:ogross@ucsd.edu">Get in touch &rarr;</a></span></aside><div class="updates-columns"><section aria-labelledby="news"><h2 id="news">News</h2><div class="panel-scroll" tabindex="0" role="region" aria-label="News"><div class="compact-entry"><span class="compact-date">2027</span> &ndash;
